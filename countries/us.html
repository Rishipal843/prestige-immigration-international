IMMIGRATION PATHWAYS

Study, Work, and Employment-Based Routes

Review primary US visa classifications available to eligible students, specialty professionals, and corporate transferees.

SPECIALTY OCCUPATION

H-1B visa

For eligible professionals sponsored by a US employer in specialty occupations requiring specialized knowledge and a bachelor's degree or equivalent.

INTRACOMPANY TRANSFER

L-1 visa

Enables qualifying multinational companies to transfer executives, managers, or specialized knowledge staff to a US office.

ACADEMIC STUDY

F-1 student visa

For international students admitted to an accredited SEVP-certified US college, university, or academic language program.

EMPLOYMENT-BASED

Employment-based immigration options

Preference-based permanent residence categories for eligible professionals, researchers, and employer-sponsored applicants.
