IMMIGRATION PATHWAYS

Study, Work, and Long-Term Routes

Review the primary UK visa routes available to students, skilled workers, and eligible applicants.

WORK ROUTE

Skilled Worker visa

For eligible applicants with a qualifying job offer from a Home Office-approved sponsor in an eligible role.

STUDY ROUTE

Student visa

For international students sponsored by a licensed UK education provider to study an eligible course.

POST-STUDY

Graduate route, where eligible

Allows eligible international graduates who complete a qualifying UK course to work or seek work in the UK.

SETTLEMENT

Relevant settlement pathways

Long-term residence options (such as indefinite leave to remain) for eligible applicants who meet residence and route criteria.
