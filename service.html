Detailed Immigration Services

Explore comprehensive guidelines, eligibility criteria, and documentation requirements for each of our core international visa categories.

01
VISITOR VISA

Bring Your Loved Ones Closer

Bring your loved ones to visit with streamlined visitor visa processing and support.

A Visitor Visa allows individuals to travel to another country for tourism, family or friend visits, medical treatment, or short-term non-employment business activities. It is generally issued for a short and specific duration and does not permit employment, paid work, or long-term stays in the destination country.

Applicants must submit valid travel documents, including a passport, along with proof of sufficient financial resources to cover expenses during their stay. Additional requirements may include travel history, accommodation details, return flight information, and, in some cases, an invitation letter from a host in the destination country.

Applicants must also provide clear evidence of intent to return to their home country, such as employment proof, family ties, or property ownership.

ℹ

The approval of a visitor visa is based on the applicant meeting all eligibility criteria, providing accurate and complete documentation, and is ultimately subject to the discretion of the embassy or consulate reviewing the application.

02
BUSINESS VISA

Expand Your Business Beyond Borders

Expand your business globally with our comprehensive business visa and investment solutions.

A Business Visa allows individuals to travel to another country for short-term business-related activities such as attending meetings, conferences, trade fairs, exhibitions, training sessions, or business negotiations. It is typically issued for a limited duration and strictly does not permit employment, paid work, or long-term professional engagement in the host country.

Applicants are required to submit valid travel documents, including a passport, along with an official invitation or support letter from a recognized business organization or company in the destination country.

They must also provide proof of sufficient financial means to cover travel and stay expenses, as well as evidence of strong ties to their home country, such as employment, business ownership, or family commitments, to demonstrate their intent to return after the visit.

ℹ

The approval of a business visa depends on meeting the eligibility criteria, submitting accurate documentation, and the final discretion of the embassy or consulate handling the application.

TOURIST VISA

Discover the World With Confidence

Plan your dream vacation hassle-free with our visa processing expertise and travel guidance.

A Tourist Visa allows individuals to travel to a foreign country for tourism, sightseeing, leisure, or recreational purposes. It is typically granted for a short duration and strictly does not allow employment, paid activities, or long-term residence in the host country.

Applicants are required to submit valid travel documents, including a passport, along with proof of accommodation such as hotel bookings or an invitation from a host.

They must also demonstrate sufficient financial means to cover travel, lodging, and daily expenses during their stay. Additionally, applicants need to provide evidence of intent to return to their home country, which may include employment proof, family ties, or return travel tickets.

ℹ

The approval of a tourist visa depends on the applicant's ability to meet all eligibility requirements, submit complete and accurate documentation, and is ultimately subject to the discretion of the respective embassy or consulate.

04
WORK VISA

Build Your Career on a Global Stage

Secure employment opportunities in Canada, UK, Australia, USA and other developed nations.

A Work Visa allows individuals to travel to a foreign country for employment purposes and grants legal authorization to work for a specific employer for a defined period. It is issued based on a valid job offer or employment contract from an employer in the host country.

Applicants are required to submit relevant educational certificates, professional qualifications, and proof of work experience, depending on the job role.

In many cases, the employer must act as a sponsor, fulfilling local labor and immigration requirements before the visa application can be approved. Work visas are generally granted for a fixed duration and may be renewable based on employment status and immigration rules.

ℹ

The approval of a work visa depends on the applicant meeting eligibility criteria, compliance with local labor laws and immigration regulations, submission of accurate documentation, and the final discretion of the issuing embassy or consulate.

05
STUDY VISA

Turn Your Educational Dreams Into Reality

Get admitted to top universities worldwide. We guide you through the entire application process.

A Study Visa allows individuals to travel to a foreign country to pursue academic or vocational education, including school, college, university, or professional training programs. It grants permission to enroll in a recognized educational institution for the duration of the approved course.

In most cases, a study visa does not allow full-time employment, though some countries permit limited part-time work under specific conditions.

Applicants must submit an official admission letter from a recognized institution, along with proof of sufficient financial support to cover tuition fees and living expenses.

Additional requirements typically include educational qualifications, language proficiency (if applicable), and evidence of intent to return to the home country after completing studies.

ℹ

The approval of a study visa depends on meeting eligibility criteria, compliance with the visa regulations of the host country, submission of accurate documentation, and the final discretion of the embassy or consulate.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Essential Documents Required for Immigration & Visa Processing

Prepare accurate, complete, and consistent records early so your application moves smoothly through verification and review.

Verification Readiness

Ensure names, dates, stamps, and translations match across all academic, employment, financial, and civil records.

Valid Passport

−

Your passport must be valid for at least six months beyond your intended travel date. It should have sufficient blank pages for visa stamps and must be in good physical condition.

Educational Certificates

−

Include all academic documents such as degrees, diplomas, transcripts, and mark sheets. These help verify your educational qualifications and eligibility for the selected visa category.

Work Experience Letters

−

Official experience letters from current and previous employers are required. These should clearly mention your designation, duration of employment, and key responsibilities.

Proof of Funds

−

Financial documents such as bank statements, fixed deposits, or sponsor letters are required to prove your ability to support yourself during your stay abroad.

Medical & Police Clearance

−

Medical examination reports and police clearance certificates ensure compliance with health and character requirements of the destination country.
